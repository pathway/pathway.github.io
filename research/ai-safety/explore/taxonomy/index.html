How to read this

The tree. One merged tree: problem side (areas 1-6, what goes wrong) and response side (areas 7-12, what researchers do). Area codes (MU, SH, ...) are always printed next to their colour.

Sources. 17 published taxonomies, 2008-2026, each with a short tag such as hc23. Every quoted node name is the source's own wording. A node marked from note was not fetched from the source.

Names are synthesis. Area and sub-area names and the mapping of source nodes to them are the author's judgement (made by Claude), not from any source. Ambiguous cases are listed in the detail pane.

Bars. A bar shows how many of the 17 sources place a node in that area or sub-area. Fewer sources means a thinner spot, not a less important one.

Catalog links. Environment counts come from the safety-environments catalog (primary_node, secondary_nodes). Incident counts come from model labels (D9a: problem sub-area the incident shows; D9b: response that would have caught it). All of these are model-assigned, not human-reviewed.

Reading status. Only 7 of the 51 survey papers behind this were read in full text; most are partial reads. The files are working material and not proofread.
