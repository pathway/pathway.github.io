    Unified AI-safety taxonomy: master tree with sources on every node

    A master tree of 12 areas and 70 sub-areas, built from 17 published taxonomies of AI risk. Every source node sits under the sub-area it maps to, quoted in the source's own words, so the tree shows where the schemes agree and where they differ.

    Tags. Keys such as [omo08] ... [iasr26] are bibliography tags (section 7). A * after a node name marks a node taken from the author's notes rather than fetched from the source. (also 1.2) means the same source node maps to a second sub-area. X.0 lines hold nodes that apply to a whole area, not to one sub-area.

    Names. Area and sub-area names (for example "3.1 Reward hacking, specification gaming, Goodhart") are synthesis, not the wording of any source. Quoted node names are the source's own. No node is listed unless it appears in a source; where the master mapping and a source differ on a name, the source's name is used and the difference is listed in section 6.

    Name note (2026-11-23): the name of 11.2 was widened from "Safer design patterns, non-agentic AI" to cover designs that separate objectives (drives) from prediction and capability: a fixed or inspectable objective module (LeCun 2022), a goal-free predictor (Bengio et al. 2025), or an objective checked by an external verifier. Only [gdm25], [sr25] and [sg25] support the node; the added papers are listed in safety/drives_separation/. The ratings and justifications were made under the old name and were not re-run.

    How to read a line

    - **3.2 Side effects, unintended consequences** — [amo16] "Avoiding Side Effects"; [dm18] "Avoiding side-effects"; [hu21] "4.4 Objective Proxies Can Lead to Unintended Consequences"

    Read: master sub-area 3.2 (name is synthesis) collects three source nodes, one each from Amodei 2016, DeepMind 2018 and Hendrycks 2021, quoted as the sources name them, sources in chronological order.
