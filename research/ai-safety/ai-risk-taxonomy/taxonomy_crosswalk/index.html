    2. MASTER merged taxonomy (my synthesis, not from any source)

    Design rule: one hybrid tree with two sides. Problem side (areas 1-6) catches risk-source, harm-type, mechanism and problem-list nodes. Response side (areas 7-12) catches approach, lifecycle and discipline nodes. X is a holding bucket for attributes and containers that are not topics.

    Problem side: what goes wrong

    Area Sub-areas
    1 MU Misuse and malicious use 1.1 CBRN; 1.2 cyber offence; 1.3 influence, disinformation, fraud, manipulation; 1.4 surveillance and censorship; 1.5 agents released with harmful goals, general dual-use; 1.6 open-weight tampering, harmful fine-tuning, distillation
    2 SH Societal, content and structural harms 2.1 discrimination, toxicity, unequal performance; 2.2 privacy, information hazards; 2.3 misinformation, epistemics; 2.4 human-AI interaction (overreliance, autonomy, anthropomorphism); 2.5 labour, inequality, access, creative economy; 2.6 environment; 2.7 power concentration, races, competitive pressure; 2.8 AI welfare and rights
    3 SP Specification, objectives, values 3.1 reward hacking, specification gaming, Goodhart; 3.2 side effects, unintended consequences; 3.3 reward tampering, wireheading; 3.4 whose values, pluralism, value verification; 3.5 preference and reward learning, assistance
    4 GR Generalisation, robustness, security 4.1 distribution shift, goal misgeneralisation; 4.2 safe exploration, recovery; 4.3 adversarial inputs, jailbreaks, injection; 4.4 poisoning, backdoors, tampering; 4.5 reliability, calibration, tail risk, uncertainty
    5 AG Agentic misalignment and loss of control 5.1 deception, scheming, situational awareness; 5.2 instrumental drives, power-seeking; 5.3 goal drift, self-modification, emergent goals; 5.4 corrigibility, shutdown; 5.5 loss of control, rogue AI; 5.6 novel risks of agentic systems
    6 MA Multi-agent and collective dynamics 6.1 failure modes and risk factors (miscoordination, conflict, collusion, selection pressure); 6.2 alignment targets for groups (context, social contract, plural stakeholders, learned governance); 6.3 theory (game theory, cooperation); 6.4 tools, evaluation, security for multi-agent

    4. Gaps

    4a. Master areas with thin coverage

    • 11 SB (4/17): only approach-cut taxonomies (GDM 2025, Shallow 2025, Singapore 2025, AISI 2025). No risk or problem list names a "design" problem. Reason: a design response is not a failure, so risk-cut taxonomies cannot hold it.
    • 2 SH (5/17): only harm-type and risk-source lists. No approach taxonomy names a technical response to discrimination (2.1), privacy (2.2) or environmental harm (2.6). Shallow, Anthropic, Singapore leave these out. Singapore 3.3 "Societal resilience" is the only partial match, to 2.3-2.5.
    • 6 MA (7/17): all seven are from 2023 or later. Older taxonomies are single-agent.
    • 1 MU (8/17): responses are spread (Shallow "WMD evals" at 7.2; Singapore "Dangerous capability..." at 7.2; GDM mitigations). No approach taxonomy has a "misuse" area of its own. Only GDM does.

    4b. Sub-areas covered by one or two taxonomies

    • 7.6 secure evaluation infrastructure: Singapore only.
    • 11.5 brain-like AGI: Shallow only. 12.5 labs list: Shallow only.
    • 2.8 AI welfare and rights: MIT 7.5 only.
    • 3.3 reward tampering, wireheading: Omohundro ("wireheading vulnerability") and Goodhart ("Metric Manipulation") only. No 2021+ taxonomy names it as a node.
    • 4.2 safe exploration, recovery: Amodei 2016 and DeepMind 2018 only. Absent from every taxonomy from 2021 on. The field moved from RL-native to LLM-native problems.
    • 3.2 side effects: Amodei, DeepMind, Hendrycks 2021 (4.4) only.
    • 8.4 data attribution, diffing, dynamics: Shallow, Singapore.
    • 12.2 organisational safety: Hendrycks 2023 only.
    • 2.6 environment: Weidinger, MIT only (IASR 2025 paper also).

    4c. Risk-to-response mismatch

    • Risks with no matching response in any approach taxonomy: 2.1 discrimination, 2.2 privacy, 2.6 environment, 1.4 surveillance, 2.5 labour (only Anwar names both).
    • Responses with no named risk in risk taxonomies: 9.3 data, 9.7 steering, 10.5 provenance, 11.4 disciplines, 8.4 diffing.

    How cuts interact - Risk and harm cuts name the problem but not the fix. Approach cuts name the fix but hide the problem. Master areas 1-6 vs 7-12 keep both. - One approach node serves many problems. Shallow "Evals" holds scheming (5.1), sandbagging (5.1), WMD (1.1), deception (5.1) as sub-nodes. The risk is the leaf label, the approach is the parent. - A mechanism node is narrower than a problem node. Carlsmith splits one GDM node ("Misalignment") into 5 classes and 3 prerequisites. - The same word names different levels. "Robustness" is a problem group in Amodei/DeepMind/Hendrycks, one RICE goal in Ji, and a design step (2.2.2) in Singapore. - Lifecycle cuts reorder approach nodes by time, not topic. Ji puts "Safety Evaluations" in backward alignment; Singapore puts "Audit techniques and benchmarks" in risk assessment; Shallow puts both under Evals.
