    Mappings are model-assigned, not human-reviewed. Numbers in brackets point to the bibliography. Full fields, including repository URLs and the licence evidence URL for each entry, are in catalog.csv.

    Licence and access columns were checked on 2026-11-22: one Sonnet agent opened each entry's repository, LICENSE file or dataset card, and a second agent tried to refute the result. ✓ = confirmed by both; ⚠ = corrected after the second check; (low) = only the paper page could be read. 'none stated' means no licence file was found, not that reuse is allowed. This is not legal advice.

    AI-use: most text in this catalog was generated by Claude (Anthropic) under the direction of Robin Chauhan. Entries were checked against primary pages by the model; mappings are model-assigned and not human-reviewed.
